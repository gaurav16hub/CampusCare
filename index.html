About CampusCare

CampusCare is a web-based complaint management system designed to improve communication between students and college administration.

It provides an organized platform where students can submit complaints, track their status and stay updated while administrators manage and resolve campus-related issues.

  • Easy complaint registration
  • Transparent complaint tracking
  • Efficient administration
  • User-friendly interface
